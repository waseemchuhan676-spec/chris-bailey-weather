About Our Weather Platform

Dedicated to providing accurate, real-time Kentucky and nationwide weather forecasts, Doppler radar analysis, and severe weather safety guidance.

Our Mission

Chris Bailey Weather is an independent digital weather dashboard and meteorological community resource. Our mission is to make real-time weather observations, hourly forecasts, 7-day model trends, and life-saving severe storm alerts accessible, intuitive, and fast for residents across Kentucky and the United States.

⚠️ Important Disclaimer & Attribution

This website is an independent community meteorological resource and weather information dashboard. While inspired by the esteemed regional weather legacy of Chief Meteorologist Chris Bailey in Kentucky, this digital project is independently operated and utilizes official open-access data feeds from the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) and the National Weather Service (NWS). For official broadcast updates, viewers should consult official broadcast stations. In emergencies, always follow instructions from local emergency management authorities.

Data Sources & Technology

  • National Weather Service (NWS) API: Real-time current observations, hourly forecasts, 7-day predictions, and emergency severe weather alerts (watches and warnings).
  • NEXRAD Doppler Radar Network: Live radar station data from NWS Louisville (KLVX), Jackson (KJKL), Fort Campbell (KHPX), and Paducah (KPAH).
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim: Precise geocoding and reverse geocoding for U.S. cities, towns, and ZIP codes.
