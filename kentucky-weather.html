Frequently Asked Questions: Kentucky Weather

Everything you need to know about weather in the Commonwealth.

Why does Kentucky weather change so quickly?

Kentucky is positioned where warm, humid air masses from the Gulf of Mexico collide with cold, dry air masses from Canada, creating frequent active frontal boundaries throughout the year.

What is the most active severe weather month in Kentucky?

April and May historically produce the highest frequency of severe thunderstorms, hail, and tornadoes in Kentucky, followed by a secondary severe weather peak in late autumn (November).

Where can I get live Lexington weather alerts?

Our website continuously queries the official National Weather Service API to display active tornado, severe thunderstorm, flash flood, and winter storm watches and warnings.
