Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

Chris Bailey Weather provides free weather forecasts and Doppler radar information. When you search for weather by city or ZIP code, your query is sent to geocoding and National Weather Service endpoints to display relevant local weather. If you grant browser location permissions via the "My Location" feature, coordinates are processed solely on your device to query nearby NWS stations.

2. Cookies and Analytics

We use industry-standard web analytics tools, including Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity, to analyze user engagement, page view patterns, and site stability. These services may place first-party cookies to record anonymized interaction data such as scroll depth, browser type, and country of origin.

3. Advertising Partners

Third-party advertising vendors (such as Monetag and Adsterra) may serve advertisements when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites. You may choose to disable or selectively turn off third-party cookies in your browser settings.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@chris-bailey-weather.vercel.app.
