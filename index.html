U.S. Weather Safety Guides

Use official emergency guidance when dangerous weather threatens.

⛈️ Severe Storms & Tornadoes

Know the difference between watches and warnings, monitor official alerts and move to a safe shelter when instructed.

  • Keep emergency alerts enabled.
  • Have a reliable way to receive warnings.
  • Follow local emergency-management instructions.

🌡️ Heat & Winter Weather

Extreme temperatures can become dangerous quickly. Check the forecast before outdoor activities and prepare for changing conditions.

  • Plan around dangerous heat or cold.
  • Check vulnerable family members and neighbors.
  • Follow official National Weather Service guidance.
