U.S. Weather Forecast Guide

Useful context for reading current, hourly and extended forecasts.

How to use today's forecast

Start with current temperature, feels-like conditions, wind, humidity and precipitation chance. These details help with commuting, outdoor plans and everyday decisions. Forecasts can change as new observations become available.

How to use hourly weather

The hourly forecast is useful when timing matters. Compare temperature, precipitation probability, wind and forecast conditions over the next several hours instead of relying on a single daily value.

How to use weather radar

Radar helps show where precipitation is being detected and how activity is moving. Use radar together with official watches and warnings; radar is not a replacement for emergency alerts.

Weather by ZIP code

Search a U.S. ZIP code, city or city-and-state combination to move from the national forecast to a local forecast. Clean city pages provide stable URLs for major U.S. locations.

About Chris Bailey Weather

Independent forecast information using official data sources.

Data Sources

Forecast and alert information is retrieved from the U.S. National Weather Service API. Location lookup uses OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Chris Bailey Weather is independent and is not an official National Weather Service service.

Severe Weather Safety

During dangerous weather, rely on official watches, warnings, emergency management and local instructions. Do not use this site as a substitute for emergency guidance.
