How to Read a Weather Forecast

Use current conditions, hourly details and the extended outlook to make practical plans.

How to Check Today's Weather

A useful weather forecast starts with the conditions that matter right now. The current temperature tells you how warm or cold the air is, while the feels-like value can better reflect how wind and humidity affect comfort. Humidity may make warm conditions feel heavier, and wind can make cold weather feel more severe.

Also check current conditions and the probability of precipitation. A rain chance is useful for deciding whether to carry an umbrella, adjust travel plans or move outdoor activities. Wind direction and speed can matter for commuting, boating, aviation and severe-weather awareness.

How to Read a 7-Day Weather Forecast

The 7-day weather forecast gives a broader view of changing conditions. Compare high and low temperatures, precipitation probability and daily conditions instead of focusing on a single number. Forecast periods can change as new observations and weather models become available.

For outdoor plans, look for the most comfortable temperature window, check the chance of rain or snow, and review wind conditions. The extended forecast is best used for planning trends rather than treating every detail several days ahead as fixed.

Weather Forecast by ZIP Code

Use the search box to enter a U.S. ZIP code, city and state, such as 40507, Lexington, KY or Louisville, KY. The site uses location search to find the selected place and then requests the appropriate National Weather Service forecast data for that area.

This makes it easier to move from national USA weather information to a local weather forecast when you need details for where you live, work or travel.

How to Use Weather Radar

Live weather radar is useful for following precipitation and storm movement. Radar displays where rain, snow or other precipitation is being detected and can help you see whether activity is moving toward or away from your area.

Radar colors generally represent different precipitation intensity, but the exact interpretation depends on the radar product. Use the radar together with official severe weather alerts because radar alone does not replace watches, warnings or emergency instructions.

Weather Forecast Guide

Understand current conditions, hourly weather, 7-day forecasts, radar and alerts.

Today's Weather Forecast

The current weather view is the fastest way to understand conditions at your selected location. Temperature, feels-like temperature, humidity, wind and precipitation probability can help you decide what to wear, how to travel and whether outdoor plans need adjustment. Because weather observations and forecast data update over time, the latest available information should be checked when conditions are important.

Hourly Weather Forecast

An hourly weather forecast is useful when your plans depend on changes during the next several hours. Compare temperature, precipitation probability, wind and the stated weather condition across the hourly periods. This can help with commuting, school activities, outdoor work, sports, travel and other short-term decisions.

7-Day Weather Forecast

The 7-day forecast provides a broader planning view. Look at daily high and low temperatures, expected conditions and precipitation chances rather than treating a single number as guaranteed. Forecast confidence can change as the forecast period approaches, so revisit the outlook before weather-sensitive plans.

Weather Radar

Weather radar helps you follow precipitation and storm movement. It can show whether rain or other precipitation is developing nearby and whether activity appears to be moving toward or away from an area. Radar is most useful when combined with the local forecast and official alerts rather than used by itself.

Severe Weather Alerts

Severe weather alerts communicate potentially hazardous conditions such as storms, flooding, extreme heat and other weather threats. A watch generally indicates conditions are favorable for a hazard, while a warning indicates that hazardous weather is occurring, imminent or detected. When an official warning affects your location, follow local emergency instructions and seek appropriate shelter or take other recommended action.

Weather Terms to Know

  • Current conditions: The latest available weather information for the selected location.
  • Hourly forecast: Short-term forecast periods useful for planning the next several hours.
  • 7-day forecast: A broader daily outlook for upcoming weather trends.
  • Precipitation probability: The forecast chance of precipitation for a period.
  • Radar: A visualization used to monitor precipitation and storm activity.
  • Watch: Conditions are favorable for a particular hazardous-weather event.
  • Warning: Hazardous weather is occurring, imminent or detected and action may be needed.

U.S. Weather Safety Guides

Use official emergency guidance when dangerous weather threatens.

⛈️ Severe Storms & Tornadoes

A weather watch means conditions are favorable for dangerous weather. A warning means hazardous weather is occurring, imminent or detected and action may be needed. An advisory generally communicates less severe but still potentially hazardous conditions.

  • Keep emergency alerts enabled.
  • Have a reliable way to receive warnings.
  • Move to appropriate shelter when instructed.
  • Follow local emergency-management instructions.

🌡️ Heat & Winter Weather

Extreme heat, winter storms, lightning, flooding and hurricanes can become dangerous quickly. Check the local forecast before outdoor activities, avoid floodwaters, seek shelter from lightning, prepare for rapid temperature changes and follow evacuation or emergency instructions from official authorities.

  • Plan around dangerous heat or cold.
  • Check vulnerable family members and neighbors.
  • Do not drive through flooded roads.
  • Follow official National Weather Service guidance.
