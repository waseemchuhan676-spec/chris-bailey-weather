Frequently Asked Questions: Bowling Green KY Weather

Answers to common questions about Warren County forecasting and radar.

Which NWS office issues forecasts for Bowling Green?

Bowling Green and Warren County weather watches, warnings, and forecasts are officially issued by the National Weather Service office in Louisville, KY.

Which Doppler radar covers Bowling Green?

Bowling Green is monitored by NEXRAD stations KHPX (Fort Campbell) and KLVX (Louisville), offering high-resolution dual-polarization precipitation and wind velocity data.
